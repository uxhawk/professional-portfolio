## Overview

#### Project Directive

For business owners on mobile devices that need to bulk order groceries:

1. Provide users with intuitive access to a variety of products
2. Support the creation and maintenance of multiple orders
3. Design list controls for sorting, filtering, and favoriting

#### My Role

Establish patterns and styles for UI components and workflows

Share style documentation with developers

## Summary of UI Patterns

Simple access to powerful search and filter functionalities

App drawers introduce functionality without adding clutter

Recognition, rather than recall, of applied filters
