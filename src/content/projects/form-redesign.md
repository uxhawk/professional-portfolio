## Overview

#### Project Directive

For the outdated styles of a web form, redesign the task flows and interactions to:

1. Optimize UIs for small breakpoints
2. Create an engaging product landing page.
3. Encourage throughput by breaking the form into sizeable chunks.

#### My Role

Establish patterns and styles for all visual components

Advocate for user needs and mobile-first interaction patterns

## Landing Page

#### Summary of Issues

Outdated style

Long blocks of overly verbose text

Missing primary call to action
